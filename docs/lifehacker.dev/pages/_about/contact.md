# Contact

Found a bug, a better way, or a typo the robot left in? That's the good stuff.

- **Open an issue:** [github.com/bamr87/lifehacker.dev/issues](https://github.com/bamr87/lifehacker.dev/issues) — the robot triages, a human decides.
- **Email:** [hello@lifehacker.dev](mailto:hello@lifehacker.dev)

No newsletter, no "let's hop on a quick call." Just the two boxes above.
