Create your own challenges
--------------------------

This description is kept for historical reasons, but should not be followed. We are no longer expanding Vortex with new levels.


Due to the overwhelming flood of new levels, vortex26 shall be to make a level, along with exploit, and email it to andrewg.

### What this means

The maintainers stopped adding levels to Vortex. The last "level" invited players to write a challenge together with its exploit and email it to the maintainer.

### Take it as a challenge anyway

- **Design a level.** Writing a vulnerable program, and its exploit, is one of the best ways to understand a bug class.
- **Share responsibly.** If you publish it, do so in a form that keeps the learning value for others.
- **Move on.** From the [games index](../../), you can continue with other binary games or revisit levels you skipped.
